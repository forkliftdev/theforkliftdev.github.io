The story

Built by a forklift driver.

Age is just a data type. I'm Chris Webb, a warehouse worker Monday through Wednesday, indie developer Thursday through Saturday. I'm 59 years old, I operate a forklift for a living, and I build software with a little help from AI.

FlexWord started as a Wordle clone — the classic first project — and grew into an eight-mode word game platform because stopping at "clone" felt like a waste of the idea.

CapSort came from a much smaller irritation: a screenshot library with thousands of unsorted images and no fast way to deal with them. Apple's own crop tools are unreliable enough that people have leaked uncropped screenshots into Slack by accident — CapSort exists to make that mistake structurally impossible, not just less likely.

TheForklift.dev LLC develops and publishes both. This is what happens when you don't quit.
