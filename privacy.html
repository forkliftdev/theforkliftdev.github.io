1. Overview

FlexWord is a word-puzzle game published by TheForklift.dev LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what data FlexWord collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the FlexWord app across iOS, Android, and web, and to all of its modes: Daily FlexWord, FlexStarter, FlexPlay, FlexRate, FlexArena, and FlexBard.

FlexKids is a separate app. FlexKids, our word game for early learners, is a standalone product with its own codebase, no ad or analytics SDKs, and no cloud data collection. It is covered by its own privacy policy, published separately when FlexKids ships to app stores, not by this document.

2. Data we collect

Account & authentication

If you create an account or sign in, we collect an email address (if you register with email, Google, or Apple), an optional display name, which sign-in method you used, and your subscription status. You can also play as a guest, in which case you're identified only by a random, pseudonymous ID with no personal information attached.

Gameplay data

We store your game history in our cloud database, linked to your account ID:

DataWhy we collect it
Target words, guesses, solve status, game durationYour stats dashboard, streaks, and difficulty tuning
Achievement progressUnlocking and tracking in-app achievements
Word difficulty ratings you submit (FlexRate)Crowdsourced difficulty calibration across the word list
Problem-word reports you submitDictionary quality and curation
FlexStarter pack resultsUnderstanding which starter-word combinations play well

Usage events

We log lightweight, pseudonymous events (like starting a mode, using a hint, or unlocking an achievement) via Firebase Analytics so we can see which features are actually used. These events never include your name, email, or advertising tracking identifiers.

Advertising & device data

If you're shown ads in the free version, Google AdMob processes general device information, app performance data, and approximate location to serve and measure non-personalized ads. On iOS, advertising tracking identifiers (IDFA) are disabled entirely by default. See §5 for details.

On-device data

Your in-progress game state, offline sync queue, arena rating, cached results, and custom FlexStarter packs are stored locally on your device. This is cleared when you uninstall the app and is never sent to us unless you're signed in and online.

3. How we use it

  • Run the game: sync your progress, stats, and achievements across devices.
  • Improve the word list: your ratings and problem-word reports directly shape word difficulty and dictionary curation.
  • Understand feature usage: aggregate, pseudonymous event data tells us what's working.
  • Manage subscriptions: entitlement status is synced via RevenueCat.
  • Serve and measure ads: non-disruptive ads delivered via Google AdMob.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your gameplay with other players.

4. Third-party services

Google Firebase

Handles authentication, stores your gameplay data (Cloud Firestore), and processes anonymous usage metrics. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

Manages subscriptions and links your account ID to your entitlement status. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center / Google Play Games

Optional. If you connect a platform account, only unlocked achievement names are shared, not gameplay details. The app works fully without this.

Google Sign-In & Sign in with Apple

Used only to authenticate you. We don't request access to your contacts, calendar, or location through these providers.

Google AdMob

Serves in-app ads. See §5 below.

5. Advertising & AdMob

FlexWord shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the free version of the game accessible. AdMob processes IP address, general device information, and coarse location to deliver non-disruptive ads.

Your choices

  • iOS: FlexWord does not collect your iOS Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or display Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ads served on iOS are non-personalized and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Everywhere: You can review or reset your ad personalization choices anytime in Google's Ads Settings.

Turning off personalized ads does not stop ads from showing. It means the ads you see are no longer based on your activity elsewhere.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep game history, achievements, and ratings for as long as your account exists, since they feed skill tracking and dictionary curation. Usage-event logs are kept roughly 90 days. On-device data is cleared when you uninstall the app.

To delete your account, log in to your account from the Settings page. Click on the "Delete Account" button and confirm your choice in the pop up screen. This permanently deletes your history, stats, and achievements. Ratings and word-feedback you submitted remain in our database, but are disassociated from your account. Please note: if you've set a screen name, it will remain, even after account deletion, because it's stored only on your device and never on our servers.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, request deletion, export your game history, or object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email privacy@theforklift.dev.

FlexWord's infrastructure runs on Google Cloud / Firebase, which may process data in the United States. By using FlexWord, you consent to your data being processed there.

8. Children's privacy

FlexWord is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you're looking for a game built for young players, see FlexKids, a separate, standalone app with no ads, no analytics, and no cloud data collection, designed with COPPA in mind.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information through FlexWord, contact us at privacy@theforklift.dev and we'll delete it.

9. Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2+.
  • Data at rest in Firestore is encrypted using Google-managed encryption.
  • Passwords are hashed by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them in plain text.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using industry-standard practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FlexWord adds features. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date, and significant changes will be announced in-app.
